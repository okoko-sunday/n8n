How it works

Behind our request form is an automation that reads every message, decides who should handle it, and keeps a record, all in a few seconds. Here's what happens, step by step.

Six steps, a few seconds

What happens to your message

  1. 1

    Request received

    The website sends your message to our automation (an n8n webhook) the moment you press Send.

  2. 2

    Cleaned and checked

    Extra spaces are removed, the email is standardised, and empty messages are rejected before any AI is used.

  3. 3

    AI reads it

    An AI model classifies it as Sales, Payment or Delivery, estimates how confident it is, and drafts a reply.

  4. 4

    Confidence check

    A score of 0.85 or higher is routed automatically. Anything lower goes to a human reviewer.

    ≥ 0.85 → auto-route < 0.85 → human review
  5. 5

    Recorded

    Every request is logged in a spreadsheet with its category, confidence score and status.

  6. 6

    Notified

    The right team gets an email, and you receive a confirmation that your request has arrived.

When something goes wrong

Technology sometimes fails. If it does, your request is not lost.

  • If the AI service is unavailable, your request goes straight to a human instead.
  • If the spreadsheet can't be reached, the request is still passed on to staff.
  • Our staff get an alert so they can fix the problem and follow up with you.
